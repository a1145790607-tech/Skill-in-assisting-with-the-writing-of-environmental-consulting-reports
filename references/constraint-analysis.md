# Regulatory, Planning, And Management-Constraint Evidence Chain

## 适用依据核查

编写或审查实质性合规论证时，按报告的评价时点核对依据的适用版本、发布与实施日期、替代关系、过渡期，以及地域、行业、对象和工况范围。优先核对发布机关、官方标准平台或审批许可原件；历史评价不能直接套用当前版本。无法核验时，明确哪些依据仅来自所提供材料，不声称已确认其有效性。

区分污染物排放限值、环境质量目标、排污许可要求和企业内部控制值，分别用于对应对象和判断，不交叉替代。多个要求并存或冲突时，核对其效力、适用范围及明确的衔接规定，不能机械选择数值最严者；不能确定适用依据时，指出冲突和待核事项，暂不作确定的符合性结论。

局部语言修改保留提供材料中的引用，不自动扩大为全面法规检索；发现影响当前结论的明显疑点时具体提示。以下证据链中的“要求”应建立在已明确的适用依据上。


For reasonableness, feasibility, or compliance analyses involving ecological-environment zoning ("three lines and one list"), laws and regulations, plans, standards, permits, access lists, water-function management, lake protection, government controls, or enterprise management limits, use this mandatory sequence for every material item:

> control requirement -> project fact -> comparison and judgement

- **Control requirement:** State the constraint before describing the project. Identify who issued or owns the constraint, what object and indicator it governs, its threshold or prohibited action, spatial and temporal scope, and any trigger, exemption, or approval condition. Name the applicable document, year/document number when available, management unit, permit, approved design, operating rule, capacity limit, budget, contract, or internal control basis. Do not replace the applicable requirement with a generic policy slogan.
- **Project fact:** Provide verifiable project evidence corresponding to that requirement, such as an overlay or location check, land-use designation, route and control-line relationship, design scale, process unit, concentration, pollutant load, resource source and quantity, monitoring facility, risk-control facility, approval, or inclusion in a plan. A bare statement such as "not involved", "not occupied", "not exceeded", or "not prohibited" is not evidence by itself.
- **Comparison and judgement:** Compare the project fact with the requirement explicitly. State whether the project is below, at, or above the limit and quantify the margin where possible. If it is above or outside the ordinary limit, explain reasonableness only through an applicable exemption, approved adjustment, replacement or offset, alternative control measure, or other documented basis; project need alone is not a justification. Place "complies", "is coordinated", "is reasonable", or another judgement only after this comparison. If evidence is missing, identify the missing overlay, approval, quantity, or control value instead of asserting compliance.

Treat this three-part sequence as a reasoning chain, not a mandatory three-paragraph or three-label layout. In report prose, default to a compact two-part structure for each parallel item:

1. state the control or management requirement;
2. state the project condition, perform the comparison, and end with the compliance or reasonableness conclusion.

Do not expose labels such as "project fact" or "comparison and judgement" in ordinary report text unless the user requests an audit-style table. Use natural transitions such as "the project...", "compared with this requirement...", "therefore...", or "the results show...". State each project fact, number, route, or measure once; the concluding sentence should add only the relationship to the limit, remaining margin, condition, or reason, and must not repeat the evidence. Keep parallel compliance items similar in structure and analytical scope. If every item already ends with a conclusion, omit the parent-level summary or reduce it to one non-repetitive sentence.

Apply the same order to enterprise-management constraints such as treatment capacity, hydraulic or pollutant-load limits, operating ranges, seasonal control values, emergency-storage capacity, maintenance windows, monitoring triggers, staffing, cost ceilings, and contractual service levels. Distinguish legally binding government requirements from approved enterprise controls and technical operating boundaries; do not present an internal preference as a statutory requirement.

For "three lines and one list" analyses, apply the evidence chain as follows:

- **Ecological-protection redline:** Use the redline or protected-space overlay/check result together with site land use, facility footprint, pipeline or route arrangement, and any crossing or occupation information. Do not infer redline compliance from project type alone.
- **Environmental-quality bottom line:** Start from current quality and the applicable target; then compare project concentrations, loads, regional assimilative capacity or restricted discharge total, before/after or no-project scenarios, and modelled sensitive-section responses. Identify indicators with little or no remaining margin.
- **Resource-utilization upper line:** Identify actual water, energy, land, fuel, and other controlled resources; state their source, quantity or design basis where available, whether new withdrawal or high-pollution fuel facilities are involved, and how reuse or efficiency changes the balance. Do not call consumption "small" without a comparison basis.
- **Ecological-environment access list:** State the exact control-unit name/code and the applicable space, emission, risk, and resource requirements; map each requirement to concrete project design or management measures. Use industrial classification, land-use status, process standard, monitoring, emergency diversion, secondary containment, anti-seepage, fuel type, or other evidence as applicable.

When a compact audit table is appropriate, use the columns `Control requirement | Project condition and basis | Compliance analysis`. Keep the same reasoning chain in prose without mechanically reproducing these labels. Do not create a list of self-certifying conclusions in which each paragraph only says that the project does not involve a prohibited circumstance.
