# Figure And Table Formatting


Unless the user explicitly requests another style, apply the following rules to figures and tables prepared for reports, Word documents, or presentations:

- Treat report text, figures, and tables as complementary. Let the visual carry detailed values, rankings, and patterns; Organize the accompanying prose according to the section’s role: descriptive sections summarize observed facts and essential scope; analytical sections explain supported causes and project implications only when these answer the section’s question. A chart alone does not establish causation. Do not reproduce every value or series in prose. Repeat only the numbers needed for a threshold comparison or decisive judgement, and state units, statistical scope, and essential definitions once in the appropriate caption, method, or text.
- **表格用于对照，不用于容纳大段正文。** 不要为了精简篇幅将所有信息塞入表格。来源背景、路径关系、计算过程、原因及条件等需要连续解释的内容，应按对象或主题分段叙述；表格只保留便于横向比较的简短类别、数值和必要参数。若单元格需要容纳多个完整句子或大段说明，应优先移回正文，而不是缩小字号或继续加宽表格。
- **共同维度入表，独有信息入文。** 比较多个对象时，表格列应采用各对象共有、含义和口径可对应的维度，用于比较数值或类型；各对象独有的核算对象、方法、参数和依据，分别在正文说明，不强塞进一个“计算依据”单元格，也不以大量不适用项凑成统一表。参数数值不同本身不妨碍列表，关键是参数是否具有共同含义和可比口径。例如生活污水与工业废水可对照共同系数和最终水量，而人口法、用地法及其各自用水指标宜分别用文字交代。
- Give adjacent tables distinct analytical roles. If one table already presents overall inputs, outputs, standards, or totals, a following table should present a different dimension such as stage contributions, spatial differences, temporal changes, or component efficiencies. Remove repeated columns and rows unless the repetition is required for an immediate comparison that would otherwise be unclear.
- Do not place a figure title inside a chart, flowchart, schematic, route diagram, or other figure. Put the figure number and title in the document caption outside the figure.
- Keep figure text minimal. Retain only essential object names, process steps, directions, key quantities, and necessary legends; move explanatory sentences, source notes, assumptions, and conclusions to the surrounding text or a concise figure note.
- Use a white background for flowcharts, process diagrams, relationship diagrams, route diagrams, and schematics. Do not use blue or other colored node backgrounds, colored panels, gradients, decorative fills, shadows, or ornamental borders. Prefer black or dark-gray outlines, connectors, arrows, and text.
- Format tables with single solid-line borders and a white background. Do not use blue header fills, colored cell backgrounds, double-line borders, decorative frames, gradients, or shadows.
- Keep any necessary legend or note compact and visually separate from the main flow so that the figure remains readable at report-page scale.
